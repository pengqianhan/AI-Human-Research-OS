# Paper Wiki

Shared paper notes, topic syntheses, concepts, sources, and graph navigation.

## Intake and ownership

Use the `paper-wiki-manager` skill for additions and structural updates.

- Store one reusable note per paper under `papers/`; start with the configured
  minimal reading profile and deepen it when a project question requires more.
- Use `topics/` for durable synthesis that improves retrieval, comparison, or
  next-step planning—not as a duplicate tag system.
- Use `concepts/` for recurring named methods, datasets, benchmarks, metrics,
  terms, and tools; create an entity page when it appears across papers, is a
  durable field-level concept, or the human asks to track it.
- Store blogs, documentation, talks, and other non-paper reading under
  `sources/`.
- Fetch bibliographic facts with literature-search tools and preserve unknowns
  rather than inventing metadata.

Shared understanding belongs here. Project-specific claims and BibTeX entries
belong in the project's `paper_skeleton.md` and `paper/references.bib`; a paper
page may link back through `# Used In Projects`.

## Sections

* [Papers](papers/index.md) - One OKF concept per arXiv paper.
* [Topics](topics/index.md) - Topic pages that connect related papers.
* [Concepts](concepts/index.md) - Entity pages for named methods, datasets, benchmarks, metrics, terms, and tools.
* [Sources](sources/index.md) - Non-paper reading: blogs, docs, and talks.
