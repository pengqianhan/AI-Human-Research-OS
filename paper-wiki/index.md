# Paper Wiki

This OKF bundle is a paper wiki: one page per paper, topic pages for research
themes, and concept pages for named entities, all cross-linked with reading
status and cross-paper notes.

# Sections

* [Papers](papers/index.md) - One OKF concept per paper.
* [Topics](topics/index.md) - Topic pages that connect related papers.
* [Concepts](concepts/index.md) - Entity pages for named methods, datasets, benchmarks, metrics, terms, and tools.

# Current Focus

* Agent coordination and shared context.
* Self-organizing AI research agents.
* Autonomous research and hypothesis-tree refinement.
* Open-world skill acquisition for LLM agents.
