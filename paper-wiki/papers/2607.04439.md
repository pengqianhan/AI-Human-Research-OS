# Q1 Problem Definition

* **Input (X):** an under-specified machine-learning research direction plus a retrieved evidence bundle covering recent and adjacent literature.
* **Output (Y):** one traceable, reviewer-facing research idea card, or an explicit refusal when evidence or audit conditions are inadequate.
* **Core problem:** LLMs can generate plausible research proposals, but the transition from literature to a defensible bottleneck, differentiated mechanism, prior-art check, and falsifiable idea remains weakly structured.
* **Scope:** the paper targets the “first mile” before implementation. It does not claim to automate experiment design, paper writing, peer review, or research acceptance.

# Q2 Problem Novelty

Research ideation, novelty assessment, literature-grounded hypothesis generation, and end-to-end AI-scientist workflows are established areas. The paper's distinctive move is to induce a non-parametric, failure-aware library of ideation-pattern cards from conference outcomes and use the same cards both to generate and audit a proposal.

Compared with search-and-rank systems or learned motivation-to-method policies, ResearchStudio-Idea binds literature retrieval, bottleneck diagnosis, pattern-guided generation, claim-level collision checking, and audit into a reusable skill suite. The accepted/high-citation/rejected contrast also gives the cards explicit evidence about weak instantiations, not only successful examples.

# Q3 Scientific Hypothesis

If recurring research-strategy operators and their success/failure conditions are extracted from accepted, high-impact, and rejected ML papers, then packaging those operators as evidence-grounded skills should help an LLM produce more defensible idea-stage proposals than either a bare model or a generic research-ideation skill, without sacrificing practical novelty.

The paper tests two linked claims: conference outcomes can be organized into a compact operational pattern vocabulary, and that vocabulary can improve inference-time ideation. It explicitly leaves implementation success, expert preference, and acceptance outcomes untested.

# Q4 Related Work and Key People

* **End-to-end AI scientists:** AI Scientist, AI-Researcher, Agent Laboratory, Idea2Plan, AI Scientist-v2, Google AI co-scientist, and Auto Research automate broader portions of the research lifecycle. ResearchStudio-Idea deliberately stops before experiments.
* **Search and multi-agent ideation:** VirSci, IRIS, Deep Ideation, Nova, FlowPIE, and Alien Science explore or evolve candidate ideas. IdeaSpark instead produces one audited proposal from a diagnosed bottleneck.
* **Pattern and knowledge induction:** Sci-Reasoning, MoRI, MotivGraph-SoIQ, Navigating Ideation Space, and knowledge-graph methods mine reusable structure. IdeaSpark's main distinction is the shared generation-and-audit pattern card induced from Oral, high-citation, and Reject evidence.
* **Novelty and evaluation:** NovBench, RINoBench, OpenNovelty, GraphMind, Scideator, IdeaBench, and related work assess idea novelty or quality. Scoop-Check performs retrieved, claim-level collision analysis across problem framing, mechanism, insight, and application domain.
* **Authors and affiliations:** Qihao Zhao, Yangyu Huang, Yalun Dai, Lingao Xiao, Jianjun Gao, Xin Zhang, Wenshan Wu, Scarlett Li, Yang He, Yan Lu, and Yap Kim Hui; Nanyang Technological University, Microsoft Research, National University of Singapore, and CFAR at A*STAR.

# Q5 Key Solution Idea

The system converts conference outcomes into reusable ideation operators, then uses those operators inside a gated research workflow:

1. Build a 1,947-paper corpus from ICLR, ICML, and NeurIPS (2021–2025), labeling Oral papers, a separately tracked high-citation subset, and rejected submissions.
2. Extract eight paper-level fields, then rewrite four innovation-mechanism fields into domain-agnostic strategy descriptions so clustering follows research moves rather than topic vocabulary.
3. Embed the strategy signatures and cluster them into 31 fine-grained sub-patterns; induce 15 higher-level ideation patterns and operational cards containing applicability, success conditions, failure modes, reviewer expectations, and examples.
4. Package three skills: **Paper-Search** for multi-source literature retrieval, **Scoop-Check** for claim-level prior-art collision checks, and **IdeaSpark** for the end-to-end evidence-to-idea workflow.
5. Run IdeaSpark through literature grounding, bottleneck identification, pattern selection and candidate generation, focused collision retrieval, a four-check quality gauntlet, optional bounded revision, implementability audit, deterministic rendering, and validation.

The central design principle is “literature first, vocabulary second, idea third.” Pattern frequency and acceptance statistics are used as audit context, not generation priors, to avoid homogenizing proposals toward common patterns.

# Q6 Experiment Design

The paper evaluates both the induced pattern space and the final idea-generation endpoint.

* **Corpus construction:** 1,947 unique papers from ICLR, ICML, and NeurIPS (2021–2025): 1,014 Oral, 260 high-citation labels, 722 Reject, with 49 Oral/high-citation overlaps. Only 1,074 papers have full abstracts, 716 parsed reviews, 682 introductions, and 340 meta-reviews in the initial merged metadata; a later recovery pass raises review evidence coverage for card generation.
* **Pattern discovery:** Claude Sonnet 4.6 extracts and abstracts innovation signatures; `text-embedding-3-large`, UMAP, and HDBSCAN identify 31 sub-patterns from 1,891 complete signatures; Claude Opus 4.7 induces 15 main patterns and their cards.
* **Construct checks:** cluster sweeps, embedding/abstraction ablations, paper-level multi-label tagging, domain induction, temporal and venue analyses, and a Reject-only re-clustering test assess whether the taxonomy is coherent and broadly covering.
* **Endpoint benchmark:** 100 method-agnostic seeds derived from ICLR 2026 Oral titles, with one proposal per system from IdeaSpark, a generic Opus-authored ideation skill, bare Opus 4.8, and bare GPT-5.5. Outputs are normalized for structure and length.
* **Judging:** three blind rounds per seed. An LLM-based idea-quality skill ranks the four proposals listwise; Scoop-Check retrieves prior art and rates collision-derived novelty on a five-level scale.

# Q7 Datasets and Code

* **Training/induction corpus:** public OpenReview metadata and reviews plus Semantic Scholar citation counts for 1,947 ICLR/ICML/NeurIPS papers from 2021–2025.
* **Evaluation seeds:** 100 ICLR 2026 Oral titles rewritten into method-agnostic problem directions.
* **Released artifacts:** the ResearchStudio repository includes the Paper-Search, Scoop-Check, and IdeaSpark skills; 15 ideation-pattern cards; 31 sub-pattern cards; domain and saturation assets; schemas; prompts; renderers; and deterministic validators.
* **Local availability:** this repository already vendors the ResearchStudio-Idea suite under `research-skills-hub/collected-skills/ResearchStudio-Idea/`.

# Q8 Evidence Quality

The paper provides substantial internal evidence that the induced objects are coherent: the production clustering has silhouette 0.584; every Reject-only cluster maps into the 15-pattern taxonomy at cosine 0.897–0.986; paper-level tagging assigns at least one pattern to every one of the 1,891 complete signatures; and two-pattern composition is the modal case across outcome classes.

The endpoint result is strong within its stated automated protocol. IdeaSpark scores 3.87/4 in blind listwise quality judging and wins 88 of 100 seeds, versus 2.57 for the generic skill, 2.56 for bare Opus 4.8, and 1.00 for bare GPT-5.5. Its mean novelty level is 2.92/5, close to the generic skill's 2.86 and above bare Opus's 2.32. The paper correctly notes that GPT-5.5's higher novelty score (3.73) reflects a “novel-but-empty” failure mode: vague ideas avoid precise prior-art collisions while scoring last on quality.

The evidence does not yet establish that IdeaSpark improves real research outcomes. Both judges are LLM-based skills, the generators and quality judge may share model-family biases, the seeds come only from ICLR 2026 Orals, retrieval is not backdated, and output normalization includes equations that may favor method-centric ideas. There is no expert blind study, implementation study, acceptance study, cross-discipline evaluation, or cross-backbone validation of the full workflow.

# Q9 Contributions

1. A conference-outcome corpus and analysis that turns 1,947 ML papers into 31 sub-patterns, 15 operational ideation patterns, and 28 induced research domains.
2. Evidence that accepted and rejected papers largely share a strategy space, making failure evidence useful for auditing execution rather than predicting acceptance from pattern choice.
3. A compositional view of research ideation: two patterns are modal, and 33.6% of papers use three or more, although composition size itself does not distinguish acceptance classes.
4. ResearchStudio-Idea, an open three-skill suite that couples multi-source retrieval, bottleneck diagnosis, pattern-guided generation, prior-art collision checking, audit, abstention, and deterministic validation.
5. An automated endpoint evaluation showing a large judged-quality advantage over bare and generic-skill baselines while exposing why novelty must be interpreted jointly with specificity and quality.

# Q10 Next Steps

* Run a blind expert evaluation and then test whether proposals survive implementation, empirical falsification, and peer review.
* Backdate retrieval to prevent post-seed information leakage and repeat evaluation across venues, disciplines, task types, model backbones, and judge families.
* Add rubrics and workflow routes for benchmark, systems, infrastructure, and pure-measurement contributions, which the current method-depth judge and IdeaSpark triggers under-serve.
* Test taxonomy stability across extraction prompts, random seeds, models, and future conference years; the current 15-pattern induction comes from a single Opus 4.7 call.
* Quantify whether deterministic validators and kill-switch preservation improve downstream scientific honesty rather than only format compliance.
* **Research-OS fit:** compare the vendored suite's evidence gates, abstention paths, skill contracts, and deterministic validators with this repository's idea promotion, paper-wiki, project evaluation, and human-oversight mechanisms before adopting any workflow wholesale.
